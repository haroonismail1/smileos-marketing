How it works

Dictate, review, sign.

01

Dictate

Speak the consultation as you normally would. No template to fill, no dropdown gauntlet.

02

Review

The structured draft appears field by field. Correct anything that's wrong - it's a draft, not a decision.

03

Sign

Your signature and timestamp close the note. The record is defensible from that moment.

Under the hood

What the AI actually is.

We don't believe in mystery boxes. Here's exactly what's running, and where your data goes.

Live

Azure OpenAI Whisper for transcription

Runs on Microsoft Azure OpenAI under our own subscription, with audio transcribed in the EU (Sweden). We don't train on your data; we don't keep your audio after processing.

Live

Azure OpenAI for extraction

Structures the transcript into clinical fields. Prompted with UK dental vocabulary. Output is always JSON you can audit. Safeguarded under the Microsoft DPA (EU SCCs + UK Addendum).

Live

Edit-first, AI-second

The AI's job is to give you a starting point. Your edits are the source of truth - every field is yours to change.

Limits we're honest about

What this isn't.

The clinician signs, not the model

Nothing enters the patient record unread. The note is a draft until you sign it.

No diagnostic claims

SmileOS structures what you said. It does not interpret radiographs or suggest treatment.
